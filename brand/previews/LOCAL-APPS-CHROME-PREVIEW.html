Where amber appears (≤10% rule): the EF· monogram's period, the primary-button fill, the favorited-state border/text, the hover border on default buttons, the live-usage bar fill for Claude (the "signature accent" highlight), the current-app favorite star, the footer lockup's period, and the opt-in .ef-chip.accent tint. None of these are large fills — the surfaces that do structural work (headers, cards, tables, the banner strip) stay ink/paper/mist, and the default chip is neutral so an unqualified chip never spends the accent. Semantic ok/warn/err colors are desaturated navy-and-slate-based greens/golds/reds, kept visually distinct from the amber signature (warn leans gold, not amber, per the brand rule). On light surfaces amber renders as Amber 600 (#C1731A, contrast-safe); on dark surfaces as Amber 500 (#E8912A). Contrast: the primary button uses a darker amber fill on light (#A65F13 + white, ~4.9:1 AA) and flips to Amber 500 with ink text on dark (~7:1) — the accent is separated by lightness, not hue, in both themes.
