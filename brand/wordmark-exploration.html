5 · Proposed color tokens

The lockable system — navy, greys, one amber. To be written into the plan once you confirm.

Ink 900
#0B1926		Deepest surfaces — deck backgrounds, footers
Ink 800
#0F2233		Primary brand color — wordmark, headlines, dark surfaces
Slate 600
#34505F		Body text on light, secondary UI
Mist 300
#9FB0BC		Muted text on dark, hairlines, captions
Paper 50
#F4F7F9		Light backgrounds — documents, web
Amber 500
#E8912A		The signature. Accents only: period, rule, one bar. ≤10% of any surface
Amber 600
#C1731A		Amber as text on light (contrast-safe); hover states
Amber 50
#FBEEDC		Warm tint for callout backgrounds on light

Rules: navy and greys do all the structural work; amber never fills large areas and is never body text; on light surfaces amber text uses 600, not 500. No second hue — if a diagram truly needs one, a desaturated slate variant, never teal.

What to pick: ① a typeface (rec: IBM Plex Sans) · ② a lockup (rec: T1) · ③ a monogram direction (rec: M1) · ④ confirm the token system above. Then the identity locks and the one-pager gets built on it.
