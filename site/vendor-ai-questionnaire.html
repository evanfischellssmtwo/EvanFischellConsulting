Tell us how your AI actually works.

A short questionnaire we send before a purchase decision. It is deliberately non-technical: we want to understand what is under the hood and how it is priced, not audit your architecture. If your product has more than one AI feature, add each one — they often differ. If a question genuinely does not apply to you, mark it Not applicable and tell us why in a line. We do expect you to know your own product, so please have the right person answer rather than guessing.
